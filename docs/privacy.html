Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

CasitaCal is a personal app used only by members of one household. This page explains what it does with data.

Google Calendar data

When you sign in with Google, CasitaCal asks for permission to view your calendars and to view and edit events. It uses that access only to show events on the household iPad and to create, change or delete events that you add or edit in the app.

Calendar data is sent directly between your device and Google. It is cached on your own device so the app can start quickly and work offline. It is never sent to any server run by the developer, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising or to train AI models.

CasitaCal's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Chores, lists and meals

These are stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, and shared only with household members you invite.

Removing access

You can sign out in the app's Settings, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app removes all locally cached data.
